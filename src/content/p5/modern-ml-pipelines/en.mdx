## Learning goals

This lesson (~**8 hours**: 3h architecture + 4h mini-pipeline + 1h optional Qlib) builds a **reproducible ML quant pipeline**: ridge vs GBM under purged CV, importance (MDI/MDA/SHAP), calibration and bet sizing, and a Qlib-style Alpha158 + LGBModel mirror in pandas. Prereqs: `ml-leakage-control`, `data-cleaning`.

**Goals:** feature→model→prediction→simple portfolio sketch; trees/linear before deep nets; optional Qlib workflow reading.

**Must-teach:** versionable pipelines beat flashy models; Qlib Data/Model/Portfolio/Analysis; MDI/MDA/SHAP + substitution; calibration + AFML ch.10 sizing; public data ≠ edge; zero-shot TSFMs often trail trees (verified papers).

## Parameters

- Purged OOF: `N_FOLDS=5`, `HORIZON=5`, `EMBARGO=5`; train-only `StandardScaler`
- Models: Ridge (`alpha` in `{0.01,1,100}`); LightGBM (fallback: HistGBM)
- Portfolio: CS quintile L/S + shared P2/P4 cost stack
- DSR: Bailey–LdP with `n_trials=4` (= configs tried)
- SHAP: used if `shap` imports (lab reports honestly otherwise)

## Feature importance and substitution

Fit on a purged train fold; MDA on the purged test fold. Add `mom_20_dup = mom_20 + noise`.

Lab: MDI splits mass between `mom_20` and `mom_20_dup` (**substitution effect**). MDA/SHAP similarly share credit — correlated duplicates dilute single-feature scores.

## Qlib-style workflow (pandas)

Official Qlib docs: `Alpha158` DataHandler → `DatasetH` segments → `LGBModel.fit` → `SignalRecord` ([model docs](https://qlib.readthedocs.io/en/latest/component/model.html)).

Plain-pandas mirror: feature stack ≈ Alpha158; purged segments ≈ DatasetH; LightGBM/Ridge ≈ LGBModel; `pred_to_ls_pnl` ≈ portfolio from scores. This lab cites the docs and mirrors stages — it does not require a local Qlib install.

## Frontier: TSFMs / transformers (verified)

We **cite**, do not re-train, these 2024–2026 evaluations (arXiv checked):

- **arXiv:2511.18578** *Re(Visiting) Time Series Foundation Models in Finance* — zero-shot Chronos/TimesFM underperform CatBoost/LightGBM on daily excess returns; fine-tuning often fails to close the gap.
- **arXiv:2412.09880** *Financial Fine-tuning a Large Time Series Model* — baseline TimesFM unsatisfactory on price prediction before finance continual pre-training.
- **arXiv:2606.27100** *Pretrained Time-Series Foundation Models for Financial Return Forecasting* — ranking wins possible, but gains vs random walk are small/sparse.

**LLM agents stay in P8.** Same anti-leakage protocol would still apply to any deep sequence model.

## Expected-value framing

A versioned purged pipeline that reports near-zero IC on a weak panel is more valuable than an unpurged GBM with pretty accuracy. DSR with explicit `n_trials` keeps config shopping honest.

## Look-ahead warnings

- Train-only scalers and purged folds (from lesson 01) remain mandatory.
- Importance computed with test-fold leakage is invalid — MDA uses the purged test fold only after train fit.
- Calibration isotonic fit must not peek at the evaluation slice.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Picking the model by accuracy or train loss.
2. Ignoring DSR / trial count across alphas and trees.
3. Reading MDI without a correlated-duplicate check.
4. Sizing bets from uncalibrated probabilities.
5. Treating zero-shot TSFMs as free alpha without reading the finance evals.
