## Case (c): IC recovered, costs wipe net SR

| Config | mean daily CS RankIC | net SR | DSR |
|--------|----------------------|--------|-----|
| purged ridge | ≈ **+0.040** | ≈ **−0.38** | ≈0 |
| purged GBM | ≈ +0.034 | ≈ −1.47 | ≈0 |

This is **not** “purged IC should be ~0.” It is: **(b)** the weak signal is visible under purge, and **(c)** turnover×costs erase the net edge. Contrast with lab 01 **(a)** leak illusions that inflate IC without honest OOS design.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Headline pooled Spearman instead of mean daily CS IC.
2. Reading negative net SR as “no signal” when CS IC is still ~+0.04.
3. Skipping substitution checks on importance.
4. Sizing from uncalibrated probabilities.
5. Mythologizing zero-shot TSFMs.
