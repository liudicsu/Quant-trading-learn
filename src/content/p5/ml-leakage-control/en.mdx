## Learning goals

This lesson (~**14 hours**: 5h AFML concepts + 7h labs + 2h write-up — HEAVY) builds **anti-leakage ML** for finance: labels, purged CV with embargo, train-only scalers, and an overfitting trap. Prereqs: `factor-workflow`, `backtest-traps`.

**Goals:** demo shuffle leakage; time-split with shifted/causal features; evaluate with IC not accuracy.

**Must-teach:** failures are usually in the pipeline; never shuffle time; full purged K-fold + embargo (AFML ch.7); scalers fit on the train fold only; forward / triple-barrier / meta-labels (AFML ch.3) with look-ahead checks; score with IC / net SR / DSR.

## Parameters

- Panel: P4 weak, **50** assets × 750 days; `HORIZON=5`; `N_FOLDS=5`; `EMBARGO=5`
- Features (as of `t`): `mom_1/5/10/20`, `vol_5/20`, `rank_mom20` from returns ≤ `t`
- Labels: forward sum `t+1..t+h`; triple-barrier `pt=sl=0.02`, vertical `h`; meta-label vs `sign(mom_20)`
- Models in demos: Ridge; overfit trap = `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` + 40 noise features
- Eval: **RankIC** (not accuracy)

## Labels (AFML ch.3)

**Forward return:** `fwd_ret[t] = sum(rets[t+1..t+h])`.

**Triple-barrier:** walk the path from `t+1`; hit +pt / −sl or vertical at `h`. Lab sides ≈ balanced.

**Meta-labeling:** primary side from `mom_20[t]`; `meta_y=1` iff side agrees with barrier outcome (trade or skip).

**Look-ahead audit:** features stop at `t`; barriers read only the future path for `y`; purge later removes train rows whose label span overlaps the test block.

## Leakage control (AFML ch.7)

| Protocol | RankIC (lab) | Role |
|----------|--------------|------|
| Shuffle split | ≈0.062 | **LEAK** — forbidden |
| Global scaler on train+test | ≈0.010 | **LEAK** pattern |
| Unpurged shuffled KFold | ≈0.065 | **LEAK** — label overlap |
| Train-only scaler + time split | ≈0.010 | cleaner |
| **Purged K-fold + embargo** | ≈−0.008 | **CLEAN** |
| Walk-forward expanding | ≈0.015 | **CLEAN** |

Purge drops train times whose label span `[t+1, t+h]` overlaps the test block; embargo removes `EMBARGO` bars after the test end. Scalers fit **only** on the train fold.

![Leak vs clean](/p5/leak_vs_clean_ic.png)

## Overfit trap

Deep GBM + **40 noise features**: train RankIC ≈ **0.40**, OOS RankIC ≈ **0.01** (daily CS IC train ≈0.34 vs OOS ≈0.02). High in-sample IC is not an edge — log the trial count.

![Overfit trap](/p5/overfit_trap_ic.png)

## Expected-value framing

On a weak panel, honest purged IC near zero is the right answer. Inflated IC from shuffle/unpurged CV is a **pipeline mirage**, same family of mistakes as P2 backtest traps.

## Look-ahead warnings

- No same-bar label leakage into features.
- No scaler fit that sees the test fold.
- No CV fold that keeps train labels overlapping test times.
- Meta-labels still need purge if the barrier span crosses the fold edge.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Shuffling rows of a time-series panel.
2. Fitting StandardScaler on the full sample.
3. Sklearn KFold without purge/embargo on label horizons.
4. Celebrating train IC from noisy deep trees.
5. Scoring with accuracy instead of IC / net SR.
