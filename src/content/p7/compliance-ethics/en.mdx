## China — programmatic trading

### CSRC 《证券市场程序化交易管理规定（试行）》

- **Source (fetched OK):** https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11426/202406/content_6959680.html — 中国证监会公告〔2024〕8号
- **Verified:** published announcement dated 2024-05-11; **effective 2024-10-08**. Defines programmatic trading as orders automatically generated or routed by computer programs on exchanges. Investors must report account/funds/strategy/software info; **report then trade**. Exchanges monitor abnormal behaviours (rapid cancel/replace, large continuous prints, etc.); **specific numeric thresholds are set by the exchanges**, not listed as fixed numbers in this CSRC text. HFT features are defined qualitatively; exchanges set detailed standards and may apply differentiated fees.

### SSE / SZSE implementation rules

- **SSE Q&A (fetched OK):** https://www.sse.com.cn/aboutus/mediacenter/hotandd/c/c_20250403_10776805.shtml (2025-04-03). States the SSE implementation rules take effect **2025-07-07**. HFT recognition (as stated by SSE): single account peak **≥300** declaration/cancel messages **per second**, or peak **≥20,000** per day. Describes four abnormal-behaviour categories (instant rate, frequent instant cancel, frequent push/depress, short-window large prints); detailed monitor cut-offs are exchange-operated.
- **SZSE PDF (fetched OK):** https://docs.static.szse.cn/www/lawrules/rule/trade/current/W020250403603802169453.pdf — Article 33 matches the **300 / 20,000** HFT thresholds; Article 18 lists the four abnormal types; **effective 2025-07-07**.
- **SZSE release notice (fetched OK):** https://www.szse.cn/lawrules/rule/trade/current/t20250403_612770.html — 深证上〔2025〕292号, issued 2025-04-03, effective 2025-07-07.

### CSRC 《期货市场程序化交易管理规定（试行）》〔2025〕12号

- **Source (fetched OK):** https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_12226/202508/content_7036966.html — 中国证监会公告〔2025〕12号；also announced on [csrc.gov.cn](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7564353/content.shtml) (2025-06-13).
- **Verified:** published **2025-06-13**; **effective 2025-10-09**. Futures programmatic trading = computer-generated/routed orders on futures exchanges. **Report-before-trade:** customer reports to futures company → FC verifies → exchange confirms → then trade. HFT features are qualitative in the CSRC text; **futures exchanges set numeric HFT standards**, may apply differentiated fees / cancel charges / limits. Host-colocation and seat fairness rules; abnormal cancel/replace monitoring. Pre-effective participants get **6 months** to meet Arts. 7–12 reporting. Complements securities 〔2024〕8 — do not conflate the two markets.

### Northbound Stock Connect — program-trading reporting (eff. 2026-01-12)

- **Hub (fetched OK):** [HKEX Northbound Program Trading Reporting](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Mutual-Market/Stock-Connect/Reference-Materials/Northbound-Program-Trading-Reporting?sc_lang=en)
- **Verified:** Mainland exchanges’ NB Program Trading Reporting Requirements **effective 2026-01-12**; CCEPs/TTEPs submit via HKEX ECP from that date; existing investors had a transition window through **2026-04-10**. SSE/SZSE thresholds (computed separately) include high automation of order elements, fast order rate, many stocks + high turnover, or customized software — **TWAP/VWAP-only simple splitting is generally not the trigger**. Primary texts live on SSE/SZSE; HKEX page is the English hub. Complements onshore securities 〔2024〕8 / futures 〔2025〕12 — Stock Connect northbound has its **own** reporting channel.


## United States — market access, shorts, margin, algo supervision

### SEC Rule 15c3-5 (Market Access Rule)

- **Source (fetched OK):** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240/subject-group-ECFRc8401dcba174f73/section-240.15c3-5
- **Verified:** broker-dealers with market access must maintain pre-trade **credit/capital** and **erroneous-order** controls; regulatory controls (restricted securities, authorised users, immediate post-trade reports); controls under the BD’s **direct and exclusive** control (limited allocation); **at least annual** review and **CEO certification**.

### Regulation SHO — Rule 201 circuit breaker

- **Source (fetched OK):** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-242/subject-group-ECFR1607681c7b4f78d/section-242.201
- **Verified:** if a covered security falls **10%** or more from the prior day’s listing-market close, trading centers must prevent short sales at or below the NBB for the **remainder of that day and the next** (subject to stated exceptions, including certain “short exempt” marks).

### FINRA Rule 4210 and the former pattern day trader framework

- **Rule 4210 (fetched OK):** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/4210 — current text includes **intraday margin level (IML)** definitions and deficit/satisfaction rules in (d)(2).
- **Regulatory Notice 26-10 (fetched OK):** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10 — published **2026-04-20**; amendments **effective 2026-06-04** with member phase-in through **2027-10-20**. Explicitly **replaces** the prior day-trading margin regime, including the **pattern day trader** designation counts and the **$25,000** PDT minimum equity, with intraday margin standards. Teach the **historical PDT idea** as legacy context, and the **current IML framework** as the live Rule 4210 approach as of the check date.

### FINRA Regulatory Notice 26-11 (IMD / IML interpretations)

- **Source (fetched OK):** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-11 — published **2026-05-19**; **effective 2026-06-04** (same day as 26-10 go-live).
- **Verified:** publishes / updates Rule 4210 interpretations for the new intraday margin standards — including how to compute **IMD** (intraday margin deficit) and **IML** (intraday margin level), deposits, market moves, and liquidation examples — and **deletes** obsolete PDT day-trading interpretations. Short update next to 26-10: 26-10 = rule change; 26-11 = how members implement IMD/IML in practice + investor education links.



## SEC withdrawal of PDA conflicts proposal (S7-12-23) — awareness

On **2025-06-17** the SEC published a Federal Register notice [withdrawing several 2022–2023 proposed rulemakings](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/06/17/2025-11110/withdrawal-of-proposed-regulatory-actions), including **Conflicts of Interest Associated With the Use of Predictive Data Analytics by Broker-Dealers and Investment Advisers** (File No. **S7-12-23**; originally 88 FR 53960).

**Teach carefully:** **withdrawn ≠ no remaining duties.** Advisers Act fiduciary duties, Reg BI, firm policies, and other existing rules still apply. If the Commission pursues related topics later, it would repropose. **Not legal advice.**

### FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-09

- **Source (fetched OK):** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/15-09 — **2015-03-26**. Guidance on supervising algorithmic strategies: risk assessment, code change control, testing, trading-system monitors, compliance communication; references 15c3-5 and Reg SHO.

## Crypto — high-level official status only

### China — PBoC et al. 银发〔2021〕237号

- **Source (fetched OK):** https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-10/08/content_5641404.htm — dated **2021-09-15**.
- **Verified high-level points:** virtual currencies are **not** legal tender; related businesses (exchange between fiat and virtual currency, crypto-to-crypto, dealing as central counterparty, info intermediary/pricing, token offerings, crypto derivatives, etc.) are characterised as **illegal financial activities** and prohibited; offshore platforms serving PRC residents likewise; civil acts contrary to public order may be void; financial institutions must not provide related services.

### United States — SEC/CFTC staff statement

- **Source (fetched OK):** https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/sec-cftc-project-crypto-090225 — **2025-09-02** SEC–CFTC **staff** joint statement (Project Crypto / Crypto Sprint).
- **Verified:** staff view that current law does **not prohibit** SEC- or CFTC-registered exchanges from facilitating **certain** spot crypto asset products; the statement itself warns it is **not** a Commission rule and creates **no new obligations**. Treat as a directional staff note, not a licence.

## SEC FY2026 Exam Priorities + FINRA 2026 GenAI / Agents — awareness

Complements the PDA-conflicts proposal **withdrawal** (S7-12-23): **withdrawal ≠ no remaining duties.**

- **[SEC Division of Examinations FY2026 Priorities](https://www.sec.gov/about/reports-publications/2026-examination-priorities)** (page stamped ~2025-11-17; also [PDF](https://www.sec.gov/files/2026-exam-priorities.pdf)): Emerging Financial Technology focus — automated investment tools, AI, trading algorithms/platforms, alt-data. Exams may check fair/accurate AI representations; ops vs disclosures; algo advice vs investor profiles; policies supervising AI in fraud/AML/trading/back-office.
- **[FINRA 2026 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report — GenAI / AI Agents](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/guidance/reports/2026-finra-annual-regulatory-oversight-report/gen-ai)** (**NEW FOR 2026**): rules (incl. FINRA Rule 3110 Supervision) remain technology-neutral. Emerging agent themes: autonomy, scope/authority, auditability, data sensitivity, domain knowledge, reward misalignment, plus GenAI bias/hallucination/privacy. Consider human-in-the-loop, access/data monitoring, action logging, guardrails.

**Awareness only — not legal advice.** Date-stamp official pages when you cite them. Lab row count: `n_sources=18` in `examples/p7/02_compliance_sources.py`.


## SFC Circular 24EC55 — GenAI language models (HK)

Asia peer to FINRA GenAI themes: [SFC Circular 24EC55](https://apps.sfc.hk/edistributionWeb/gateway/EN/circular/doc?refNo=24EC55) (*Circular to licensed corporations — Use of generative AI language models*), issued **2024-11-12**, immediate effect (official page fetched).

**Awareness (not a license, not legal advice):**

- Applies to licensed corporations using GenAI LMs (in-house, group, third-party, or open-source) in regulated activities.
- Risk-based Core Principles: senior management; AI model risk management; cybersecurity & data; third-party provider risk.
- **High-risk use cases** — providing investment recommendations, advice, or research — need extra mitigations including **human-in-the-loop** review before client-facing output, validation / ongoing monitoring, prompt-robustness testing, and ongoing disclosures that the user is interacting with AI.
- Complements FINRA 2026 GenAI/Agents supervision themes and SEC FY2026 Emerging FinTech exam focus: **withdrawal of a US proposal ≠ absence of duties elsewhere**.

Lab row: `sfc_24ec55` in `examples/p7/02_compliance_sources.py` (`n_sources=18`).


## Common mistakes / pitfalls

- Assuming PDA withdrawal erased all AI/advice duties (FY2026 exams still list Emerging FinTech).
- Treating FINRA GenAI themes as a trading license or product alpha.
- Treating SFC 24EC55 as a trading license rather than licensed-corporation expectations.

1. Quoting blog thresholds instead of exchange/CSRC text.
2. Treating staff statements as final Commission rules.
3. Ignoring that PDT/$25k was replaced (Notice 26-10 / 26-11 IMD/IML) if teaching US retail margin in 2026+.
4. Conflating securities 〔2024〕8 with futures 〔2025〕12 — markets and exchanges differ.
5. Offering personalised trading advice from this course.
6. Skipping the “date checked” stamp.
