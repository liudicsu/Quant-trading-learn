## US main line: the PDT successor rule — four dates + phase-in status

**Intuition:** a rule being “effective” does not mean “your broker and your account have already switched.” FINRA gave members a phase-in period, so record **dates** and **member adoption status** as two separate dimensions.

| Item | Date ([FINRA 26-10](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10), checked 2026-11-08) |
|------|------|
| SEC approval (Release 34-105226) | 2026-04-14 |
| Notice published | 2026-04-20 |
| Rule effective | 2026-06-04 |
| Member phase-in ends | 2027-10-20 |

The new intraday margin standards **replace in their entirety** the old PDT day-trade count and the $25,000 minimum-equity requirement; [26-11](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-11) (published 2026-05-19, effective 2026-06-04) gives the IML/IMD interpretations. Ordinary maintenance margin has not disappeared.

**Correct version exercise (per review item R04):** the FINRA example records, separately, the SEC approval date, the Notice publication date, the rule effective date and the phase-in end date, and adds `member_adoption_date` / `broker_checked_on` plus the applicable account category. Between 2026-06-04 and 2027-10-20, do not assert from the trade date alone that a member/account has migrated; when migration status is unknown, output `needs_broker_verification`. A local toy must not give any legal-compliance guarantee.

## United States — market access, shorts, margin, algo supervision

### SEC Rule 15c3-5 (Market Access Rule)

- **Source (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-240/subject-group-ECFRc8401dcba174f73/section-240.15c3-5
- **Verified:** broker-dealers with market access must maintain pre-trade **credit/capital** and **erroneous-order** controls; regulatory controls (restricted securities, authorised users, immediate post-trade reports); controls under the BD’s **direct and exclusive** control (limited allocation); **at least annual** review and **CEO certification**.

### Regulation SHO — Rule 201 circuit breaker

- **Source (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-242/subject-group-ECFR1607681c7b4f78d/section-242.201
- **Verified:** if a covered security falls **10%** or more from the prior day’s listing-market close, trading centers must prevent short sales at or below the NBB for the **remainder of that day and the next** (subject to stated exceptions, including certain “short exempt” marks).

### FINRA Rule 4210 and the former pattern day trader framework

- **Rule 4210 (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/4210 — current text includes **intraday margin level (IML)** definitions and deficit/satisfaction rules in (d)(2).
- **Regulatory Notice 26-10 (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10 — published **2026-04-20**; amendments **effective 2026-06-04** with member phase-in through **2027-10-20**. Explicitly **replaces** the prior day-trading margin regime, including the **pattern day trader** designation counts and the **$25,000** PDT minimum equity, with intraday margin standards. Teach the **historical PDT idea** as legacy context, and the **current IML framework** as the live Rule 4210 approach as of the check date.

### FINRA Regulatory Notice 26-11 (IMD / IML interpretations)

- **Source (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-11 — published **2026-05-19**; **effective 2026-06-04** (same day as 26-10 go-live).
- **Verified:** publishes / updates Rule 4210 interpretations for the new intraday margin standards — including how to compute **IMD** (intraday margin deficit) and **IML** (intraday margin level), deposits, market moves, and liquidation examples — and **deletes** obsolete PDT day-trading interpretations. Short update next to 26-10: 26-10 = rule change; 26-11 = how members implement IMD/IML in practice + investor education links.



### FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-09

- **Source (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/15-09 — **2015-03-26**. Guidance on supervising algorithmic strategies: risk assessment, code change control, testing, trading-system monitors, compliance communication; references 15c3-5 and Reg SHO.

## Cross-border identity boundaries: tax / FX / CRS literacy box (readers resident in China)

<Callout type="warn" title="Not tax or legal advice">
Literacy only: status, tax residence and beneficial ownership decide which rules apply. This lesson does not determine your status, compute your tax, or collect any tax ID. Checked 2026-11-08.
</Callout>

**W-8BEN and dividend withholding:** the [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw8ben): a foreign individual gives Form W-8BEN to the **withholding agent/payer**, not to the IRS; foreign persons are generally subject to US tax at 30% on certain US-source income (including dividends), which a treaty may reduce. The [US–China treaty](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/china.pdf) Article 9: when the recipient is a resident of the other state and the beneficial owner, source-state tax on dividends shall not exceed 10% of the gross amount (see also the [IRS treaty page](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/claiming-tax-treaty-benefits)). **Chinese nationality ≠ Chinese tax residence ≠ automatic treaty eligibility**; withholding is not the final settlement of all tax.

**Wash sale (per review item R25):** where the relevant US income-tax rules apply, acquiring substantially identical stock or securities by purchase or taxable exchange, or entering into a contract or option to acquire them, within the period from 30 calendar days before to 30 calendar days after the loss sale or disposition (including the sale date, 61 calendar days in total) may trigger the loss-deduction limit in [IRC §1091](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t26/s1091/). It is not a trading ban and not a guaranteed tax exemption; the general basis adjustment of the replacement securities etc. follows IRS rules ([Pub. 550](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550)), and you cannot claim every case is merely a deferral.

**China individual income tax (per review item R28):** a resident individual’s domestic and foreign income is taxed under China’s [Individual Income Tax Law](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfw/zcfgk/grsds/201809/t441789.html) (an individual with a domicile in China, or without a domicile but residing in China for a cumulative 183 days in a tax year, is a resident individual); cases such as individuals without a domicile are checked under the applicable rules, not judged by nationality alone. Income from property transfer is taxed at a 20% proportional rate, and taxable income is generally transfer proceeds minus the original value of the property and reasonable expenses; interest, dividends and bonuses also carry a 20% statutory proportional rate, with reliefs/treaties and income character checked separately. Income tax actually and lawfully paid abroad can be credited within the prescribed limit, not as an unconditional full credit; the qualifying excess may be carried forward for the next five tax years ([MOF/SAT Announcement 2020 No. 3](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-01/22/content_5471604.htm)). A resident individual with foreign income generally files between 1 March and 30 June of the following year. US withholding being complete does not imply no Chinese filing or no additional Chinese tax.

**CRS (per review item R29):** CRS is a framework for due diligence on financial accounts and reporting/exchange of tax information under applicable exchange relationships; it is not a new securities tax and does not mean every country and every account exchanges automatically. Check the applicable rules by the account’s financial institution and your tax residence, and give a truthful tax-residence self-certification; CRS and the US FATCA are different mechanisms ([SAT AEOI page](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/aeoi_index.html), [Q&A](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810341/n810760/c2620533/content.html)). Whether exchange happens does not change the duty to report foreign income lawfully; never promise that a “US brokerage account is invisible / exempt from filing.”

**FX:** the SAFE Shanghai FAQ ([2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shanghai/2026/0402/2412.html)): the annual facilitation quota for personal FX settlement and for domestic individuals’ FX purchase is USD 50,000 equivalent per person per year. The SAFE Shaanxi FAQ ([link](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shaanxi/2018/1204/249.html)): FX bought within the quota and remitted abroad needs a genuine, lawful current-account purpose, which **does not include investing in overseas securities**. So the USD 50,000 quota is **not** a licence for overseas securities investment. This lesson provides no remittance routes, splitting, nominee or false-purpose schemes.

## Comparison reading: mainland A-shares / Stock Connect — programmatic trading (extension, not the main line)

### CSRC 《证券市场程序化交易管理规定（试行）》

- **Source (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11426/202406/content_6959680.html — 中国证监会公告〔2024〕8号
- **Verified:** published announcement dated 2024-05-11; **effective 2024-10-08**. Defines programmatic trading as orders automatically generated or routed by computer programs on exchanges. Investors must report account/funds/strategy/software info; **report then trade**. Exchanges monitor abnormal behaviours (rapid cancel/replace, large continuous prints, etc.); **specific numeric thresholds are set by the exchanges**, not listed as fixed numbers in this CSRC text. HFT features are defined qualitatively; exchanges set detailed standards and may apply differentiated fees.

### SSE / SZSE implementation rules

- **SSE Q&A (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.sse.com.cn/aboutus/mediacenter/hotandd/c/c_20250403_10776805.shtml (2025-04-03). States the SSE implementation rules take effect **2025-07-07**. HFT recognition (as stated by SSE): single account peak **≥300** declaration/cancel messages **per second**, or peak **≥20,000** per day. Describes four abnormal-behaviour categories (instant rate, frequent instant cancel, frequent push/depress, short-window large prints); detailed monitor cut-offs are exchange-operated.
- **SZSE PDF (checked 2026-11-04):** https://docs.static.szse.cn/www/lawrules/rule/trade/current/W020250403603802169453.pdf — Article 33 matches the **300 / 20,000** HFT thresholds; Article 18 lists the four abnormal types; **effective 2025-07-07**.
- **SZSE release notice (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.szse.cn/lawrules/rule/trade/current/t20250403_612770.html — 深证上〔2025〕292号, issued 2025-04-03, effective 2025-07-07.

### CSRC 《期货市场程序化交易管理规定（试行）》〔2025〕12号

- **Source (checked 2026-11-04):** https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2025/issue_12226/202508/content_7036966.html — 中国证监会公告〔2025〕12号；also announced on [csrc.gov.cn](https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7564353/content.shtml) (2025-06-13).
- **Verified:** published **2025-06-13**; **effective 2025-10-09**. Futures programmatic trading = computer-generated/routed orders on futures exchanges. **Report-before-trade:** customer reports to futures company → FC verifies → exchange confirms → then trade. HFT features are qualitative in the CSRC text; **futures exchanges set numeric HFT standards**, may apply differentiated fees / cancel charges / limits. Host-colocation and seat fairness rules; abnormal cancel/replace monitoring. Pre-effective participants get **6 months** to meet Arts. 7–12 reporting. Complements securities 〔2024〕8 — do not conflate the two markets.

### Northbound Stock Connect — program-trading reporting (eff. 2026-01-12)

- **Hub (checked 2026-11-04):** [HKEX Northbound Program Trading Reporting](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Mutual-Market/Stock-Connect/Reference-Materials/Northbound-Program-Trading-Reporting?sc_lang=en)
- **Verified:** Mainland exchanges’ NB Program Trading Reporting Requirements **effective 2026-01-12**; CCEPs/TTEPs submit via HKEX ECP from that date; existing investors had a transition window through **2026-04-10**. SSE/SZSE thresholds (computed separately) include high automation of order elements, fast order rate, many stocks + high turnover, or customized software — **TWAP/VWAP-only simple splitting is generally not the trigger**. Primary texts live on SSE/SZSE; HKEX page is the English hub. Complements onshore securities 〔2024〕8 / futures 〔2025〕12 — Stock Connect northbound has its **own** reporting channel.

**Facts (verified on the [SSE official site](https://www.sse.com.cn/lawandrules/sselawsrules2025/stocks/exchange/c/c_20260424_10816482.shtml)):** the Shanghai Stock Exchange issued the *Trading Rules of the Shanghai Stock Exchange (2026 Revision)*, document no. **SSE Fa [2026] No. 41 (上证发〔2026〕41号)**, published 2026-04-24, **effective from 2026-07-06**; the *Trading Rules (2023 Revision)* published 2023-02-17 (上证发〔2023〕32号) are **repealed at the same time**. | Version | Document no. | Published | Applicable window (only the verified part) |
|------|------|------|------|

**Why a backtest cares:** price limits, order entry and trading sessions decide “can this fill today, and at what price.” When a backtest spans 2026-07-06, **pick the rule version in force on each trade date**, instead of applying today’s rules to all of history. Deferred provisions should **not** enter a backtest before an official implementation date is announced.
