## Learning goals

This lesson (~**17 hours**: 3h design + 6h running/logging + 3h parity checklist/asserts + 1h template + 1h infra name-map + 2h US broker paper differences & adapter fixture + 1h Shanghai-timezone reconciliation) builds a **paper-trading loop on synthetic data**: signal → target → simulated fill → log → reconcile → drift metrics, plus kill switches and a numeric live-vs-backtest gap. Prereqs: `risk-systems-lab`.

**Goals:** CSV/sim paper matching; emit daily logs; explain what paper validates (process, not alpha).

**Must-teach:** freeze the strategy version during paper; do not edit rules for P&L; reconciliation and anomaly logs; decompose live−backtest gaps (slippage, fills, latency, data); kill switches are engineering. **No real broker order code** — this site stays learning-only.

## Parameters

- Synth series: 400 business days; signal `sign(mom20)`; vol-target 10% ann.; frozen `VERSION=mom20_vt_v1.0.0`
- Research foil: next-close fill, cost `5 bps · |Δw|`
- Paper foil: open fill, latency 1–2 bars, slip `2 + 8·|Δw|` bps, ~8% partial fills
- Kill rules: data gap → flat; DD soft at 4% / hard kill at 8%; gross ≤ 1.5

`examples/p7/01_paper_trading_loop.py` runs ≥360 sessions: each day logs version, target, position, equity, DD, and notes. Fills and kill events write to CSV under `examples/p7/data/`.

## Reconciliation and drift

**Independent broker ledger:** rebuild positions only from fill deltas, separately from the strategy equity list. Lab injects one **partial miss** on the broker side; reconcile against strategy intended positions and **assert the break is detected** (date printed with abs_diff &gt; 0). Comparing a series to itself always yields zero and teaches nothing.

Drift foil: Spearman(mom20, fwd_ret) early ≈ −0.13 vs late ≈ −0.09 (delta ≈ +0.04).

## Live vs backtest gap (numeric)

Same signal; only frictions change (close-to-close returns held fixed in the ladder):

| Stage | cum return (lab) | gap vs research |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|
| research_bt | ≈ −0.030 | — |
| +latency | ≈ +0.015 | timing can go either way on a path |
| +slip | ≈ −0.008 | structural cost |
| +partial | ≈ −0.016 | missed/partial fills |

Structural: research 5 bps·\|Δw\|; paper mean slip on turns ≈ 3 bps in this low-turnover foil (schedule still `2+8·|Δw|`); partial scales cost ×1/0.85.

## Backtest ↔ live parity — six defects + 10-point checklist

[arXiv:2610.21846](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21846) (*Where Backtests and Live Trading Part Ways*) documents six defects found when **one** intraday system’s live days were compared to their backtest replay — drift that **summary statistics never showed**. **Protocol only:** no PnL claim, no broker API, no advertised Sharpe.

**Six defect classes (lab synthetic asserts — all 6/6 caught):**

1. **Feed ≠ SIP / thin open volume** — default feed carried a small fraction of real opening volume.
2. **Div-adjusted vs unadjusted** — research prices adjusted for dividends that live tickets never see.
3. **Sizing past live limit** — research size exceeded the live account constraint.
4. **UTC ≠ NY** — UTC timestamps read as New York session time.
5. **Live downloads missing in replay** — files the live path fetched never entered the backtest.
6. **Config on ≠ wired** — settings switched on but never reached the order-submit code.

**10-point checklist** (engineering, not alpha): same feed class; same adjustment policy; live size caps; explicit timezone; replay includes every live artifact; risk flags wired into submit; frozen fill model; shared cost object; frozen VERSION + config hash; daily recon with pause-on-break.

Prefer this paper over live-money crypto AI backtest marketing (e.g. reject shipping 2609.34510-style claims here).

<Callout type="warn" title="Debunk">
Parity checklists do not prove edge. Catching a clock bug is process quality — not a Sharpe.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Editing signal rules after seeing paper P&amp;L.
2. Calling paper SR “live proof.”
3. Mixing research fills into the paper ledger.
4. No recon between fills CSV and equity.
5. Shipping broker API keys or order routers in the learning repo.
6. Filing US fills by Shanghai date so one fill “crosses days”; assuming Alpaca paper simulates dividends or checks book size.
