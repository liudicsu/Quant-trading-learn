## Learning goals

This lesson (~**8 hours**: 3h formulas + 4h coding + 1h quiz) implements annualized vol, Sharpe, max drawdown (MDD), and plots rolling vol plus stress-period correlation. Prereqs: pandas time series, stats basics, markets language.

## Annualized vol and Sharpe

<Formula label="Annualized vol">
σ_ann ≈ σ_day × √252
</Formula>

<Formula label="Teaching Sharpe (declare Rf)">
Sharpe ≈ (mean(r) − Rf) / std(r) × √252
</Formula>

Lesson output: σ_day≈0.0111, σ_ann≈0.176, Sharpe_Rf0≈0.664.

## Max drawdown (path-dependent)

dd = nav/peak − 1 after running peaks; MDD is the minimum. The **same returns in another order** yield another MDD — path dependence. Here MDD≈−0.108.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Annualizing vol with ×252 instead of √252.
2. Comparing Sharpes without stating Rf/frequency.
3. Reporting returns without MDD.
4. Letting full-sample corr hide stress corr spikes.
5. Treating tiny rolling windows as precise truth.
