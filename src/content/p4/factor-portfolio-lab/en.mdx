## Learning goals

This lesson (~**7 hours**: 2h concepts + 4.5h lab + 0.5h quiz) turns a cleaned factor into a **portfolio experiment**: neutralization before/after, single vs EW multi-factor honesty, turnover–cost schedules, crowding, and **strong vs weak** panels. Prereq: `factor-workflow`.

**Goals:** estimate factor-portfolio turnover; sketch size/industry neutralization impact; compare single vs EW multi-factor.

**Must-teach:** combining factors does not auto-raise tradeability; turnover and the shared cost model decide net returns; before/after neutralization; crowding and decay; weak signals need cost control and honest combination.

<Callout type="info" title="Learning only — not investment advice">
Same planted covariates. **`MU_STRONG=0.0025` is inflated teaching**; **`MU_WEAK=0.0010`** is the realistic RankIC foil. The nuisance `raw_factor2` has **no** premium.
</Callout>

## Parameters

- Reuse P4-01 dual panels; neutralize with **`log_mcap.shift(1)`**
- Quintile L/S; hold schedules 1/5/10/20 days; costs = P2 10 bps + lagged √impact
- Overlapping IC windows inflate IC autocorr / ICIR SE (not mean IC) — non-overlap or Newey–West

## Neutralization before / after

Strong RankIC: before ≈ **0.076** → after ≈ **0.112**. Nuisance after neut ≈ **−0.003**. Covariates: industry + **lagged** mcap only.

## Strong vs weak (portfolio metrics)

| Panel | MU | RankIC | ICIR | gross SR | net SR | mean turn |
|-------|-----|--------|------|----------|--------|-----------|
| strong (inflated) | 0.0025 | ≈0.112 | ≈4.64 | ≈4.50 | ≈3.67 | ≈1.06 |
| weak (realistic) | 0.0010 | ≈0.043 | ≈1.77 | ≈1.62 | ≈0.79 | ≈1.06 |

Weak RankIC is the live-like case: **costs often erase most of the gross edge**. Multi-factor research exists to stack many such weak signals — not to average in null noise.

![Portfolio strong vs weak](/p4/portfolio_strong_vs_weak.png)

## Single vs EW multi-factor

**Strong:** true_only net SR ≈ **3.67** vs EW+nuisance ≈ **3.09** (IC 0.112→0.079).

**Weak:** true_only net SR ≈ **0.79** vs EW+nuisance ≈ **0.23** — blending a null factor hurts more when the edge is already thin.

![NAV compare](/p4/portfolio_nav_compare.png)

## Turnover–cost table

| Hold | strong net SR | weak net SR | mean turn |
|------|---------------|-------------|-----------|
| 1d | ≈3.67 | ≈0.79 | ≈1.06 |
| 5d | ≈4.29 | ≈1.42 | ≈0.21 |
| 10d | ≈4.36 | ≈1.50 | ≈0.10 |
| 20d | ≈4.23 | ≈1.39 | ≈0.05 |

On **weak** signals, cutting turnover is often the difference between net>0 and net≈0.

## Crowding foil

Long-leg HHI ≈ **0.025**, rank AC1 ≈ **0.88**. Sticky overlapping books across funds are the crowding channel.

## Expected-value framing

Gross SR on an inflated MU is not a product. Net of the shared cost model on the **weak** panel, plus an honest null-blend test, is the minimum bar before any multi-factor upgrade claim.

## Look-ahead warnings

- Neutralize with `log_mcap.shift(1)` only.
- Freeze/hold logic must not peek at future ranks.
- Impact σ lagged; overlapping IC → non-overlap or NW for ICIR SE.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Equal-weighting every candidate without a null control (worse on weak panels).
2. Ignoring turnover when RankIC is only a few percent.
3. Neutralizing with same-day mcap.
4. Treating the strong panel as the live baseline.
5. Reporting only gross NAVs.
