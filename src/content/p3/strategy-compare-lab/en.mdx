**Goals:** three-strategy side-by-side report; judge vs baseline.

**Must-teach:** control hidden assumptions; simple is the baseline line.

## Controlled assumptions

- Single-name universe: AAA; pairs: synthetic Y_coin / BBB
- Fill: next-open via `shift(1)`; Rf=0
- Costs: 10 bps one-way (+ √impact for single-name); pairs 2× stack
- OOS: last 30% bars
- **Baseline:** dual MA(10,30)

## Hidden assumptions checklist

Universe, cost model, fill timing, Rf, OOS cut, and whether pairs uses a constructed foil must be identical or explicitly declared. Changing one quietly invalidates the horse race.

## Look-ahead / honesty

- Do not pick the “winner” by peeking at OOS then rewriting IS rules.
- Headline CAGR on open sites ≠ edge under your costs.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Different cost models per strategy.
2. Celebrating IS winners that lose OOS.
3. No pre-declared baseline.
4. Treating open-library Return% as the scoreboard.
5. Adding parameters until IS looks good.
