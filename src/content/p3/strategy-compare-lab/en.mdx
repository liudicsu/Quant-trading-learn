## Learning goals

This lesson (~**5 hours**: 1h framing + 3.5h report + 0.5h quiz) runs a **controlled horse race** under **one cost model** (including impact for pairs) and links the winner to **DSR with explicit trial count N** from P2 `backtest-traps`. Prereqs: all prior P3 lessons.

**Goals:** three-strategy (+baseline) report; judge vs baseline; compute DSR(N).

**Must-teach:** control hidden assumptions; simple is the baseline; multiple testing via DSR.

Synthetic results — not a mandate to trade any row.
</Callout>

## Controlled assumptions (one cost model)

- AAA for single-name; Y_coin/BBB for pairs
- Fill `shift(1)`; Rf=0; OOS last 30%
- **Every** strategy: P2 one-way **10 bps** stack + √impact × turnover; pairs **`n_legs=2`**
- Baseline: dual MA(10,30)
- **N = 4** explicit trials in this lab: baseline, TSMOM-skip, MR-z, pairs-rolling

## DSR on the best OOS strategy (P2 link)

Best by OOS net SR: **pairs_rolling**. Cross-trial σ_SR from the N raw daily Sharpes ≈ **0.041**. With N=**4**:

- SR_daily≈**0.0108** (ann≈0.17)
- SR* = E[max SR]≈**0.0436**
- **DSR≈0.284**

Even the relative winner fails a strict multiple-testing bar — same Bailey & López de Prado spirit as `backtest-traps`.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Different cost models per row (esp. omitting pairs impact).
2. No explicit N before DSR.
3. Celebrating IS winners that lose OOS.
4. No pre-declared baseline.
5. Adding parameters until IS looks good.
