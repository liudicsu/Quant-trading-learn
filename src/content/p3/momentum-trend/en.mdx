## Learning goals

This lesson (~**8 hours**: 3h concepts + 4.5h labs + 0.5h quiz) compares **dual moving-average trend**, **time-series momentum** with a skip window (12-1 spirit), a **cross-sectional toy**, and **10% vol targeting** — all net of P2 costs. Prereq: `engines-vectorized-event`.

**Goals:** dual-MA vs simplified momentum; report turnover and by-year results.

**Must-teach:** skipping the most recent window dampens short-term reversal bias; crowding and turnover erode edges.

<Callout type="info" title="Learning only — not investment advice">
Synthetic ~1y panel with scaled lookbacks — not a claim that momentum works live.
</Callout>

## Parameters

- Panel: AAA/BBB/CCC daily 2022-01-03 → 2023-02-24 (n=300)
- Dual MA: fast=**10**, slow=**30**; TS mom: LB_FULL=**60**, SKIP=**5** (12-1 *spirit* on a short panel, not literal months)
- Vol target: **10%** ann, vol_lb=**20**, scale clip ≤2
- Costs: P2 four-part stack **10 bps** one-way + √impact × turnover; Rf=0; `position=shift(1)`
- OOS: last **30%** of bars

## Classic sources (read & used)

- **Jegadeesh & Titman (1993), *JF*** — relative-strength J/K strategies; skip-a-week variants avoid bid-ask / short-term reversal effects ([PDF](https://bauer.uh.edu/rsusmel/phd/jegadeesh-titman93.pdf)). Industry **12-1** extends that skip idea to the recent month.
- **NYU Stern Dhar *Trading Strategies* syllabus** — sequence trend → counter-trend → spreads/pairs ([PDF](https://web-docs.stern.nyu.edu/ioms/SYLLABI/Dhar_MULT-UB.0035_Fall13.pdf)).
- Curriculum **Canvas m6** momentum template (internal map).

## Niche foil

- **[quants.md](https://quants.md/)** open library (QuantJourney, Apache-2.0) — many trend/momentum cards print Sharpe near 0 or negative; use as **hypothesis dumps**, not proof. Pine strategy packs were **not independently re-read this batch** and are dropped from claims.

## Dual-MA vs TS momentum

Both use next-open fills (`shift(1)`). Lab net Sharpes (full sample): dual-MA ≈ **−0.66**, TS-mom-skip ≈ **−0.12**, vol-targeted TS ≈ **−0.08**. OOS net SR remain negative on this toy — honest evaluation, not a victory lap.

![Momentum NAV](/p3/momentum_nav.png)

## Skip-month motive (even when the toy disagrees)

On **this** short synthetic panel, no-skip can print a higher in-sample net SR than skip. That does **not** refute the JT/industry design motive: skip reduces contamination from short-term reversal / microstructure. **Expected-value framing:** skip is a bias-reduction choice, not a guarantee on every sample.

## Cross-section & by-year

3-name CS long/short is a **toy** (lab CS net SR≈0.50) — real CS needs a broad universe. By-year net SR for TS-mom-skip: 2022 ≈ **−0.13**; 2023 incomplete → NaN.

## Look-ahead warnings

- Momentum score must use only past returns (here: end at t−SKIP).
- Never rank with the same bar’s close you trade.
- Vol scale uses `shift(1)` so today’s vol does not size today’s fill.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Literal 12-month windows on a 1-year toy panel without stating the scale.
2. Celebrating gross curves before P2 costs.
3. Treating CS on 3 names as institutional evidence.
4. Confusing “skip lost on this sample” with “skip is wrong.”
5. Using same-bar prices in the formation window you trade.
