## Half-life (AR(1) / OU)

Fit **Δx_t = a + b x_(t-1) + ε**. If −1 &lt; b &lt; 0:

`half_life = −ln(2) / ln(1+b)`

**OU form:** `dx = κ(θ − x) dt + σ dW` with `κ ≈ −ln(1+b)` per bar, so `half_life = ln(2)/κ` (same number).

Lab on AAA: b≈**−0.0123**, κ≈**0.0124**, **HL≈55.8 bars** → Z_LB=**60**, holding hint≈40. Large HL ≈ near unit root → fade edges are slow/fragile.

## z-score + no look-ahead

Intent after close[t] is stored; `position[t] = intent[t−1]`. **ASSERT** passes: a z-exit at close[t] leaves `position[t]=1` and flats from t+1. Price stops may still flatten the same bar (path-dependent).

## Stops A/B & trend failure

No-stop net SR≈**−1.97**; with-stop ≈**−1.10** (stop_exits=**4**). Drift foil keeps punishing fades.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Using z[t] to change position[t] (look-ahead).
2. Ignoring half-life when picking lookback.
3. No stop / no invalidation list.
4. Estimating ±2 on the full sample including test.
5. Claiming MR works in strong trends without a failure foil.
