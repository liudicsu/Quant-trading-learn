## Learning goals

This lesson (~**7 hours**: 2.5h concepts + 4h labs + 0.5h quiz) builds a **z-score mean-reversion** rule, compares **with/without stops**, and lists **invalidation** conditions — especially failure in trends. Prereq: `momentum-trend`.

**Goals:** implement z rules; write an invalidation list.

**Must-teach:** in strong trends, reversion dies by repeated stops; thresholds must be re-estimated.

## z-score rule

Compute rolling mean/std of price; z=(p−μ)/σ. Long when z&lt;−2, flat when z≥0. Positions enter next bar.

## Stops A/B

Lab (original panel): no-stop net SR≈**−1.32** (NAV≈0.83); with-stop net SR≈**−1.36** (NAV≈0.89, stop_exits=**5**). Stops change the path — they are not free optionality.

## Trend failure

On a **+0.15%/day drift foil**, the same long-only fade keeps hitting stops (lab stop_exits on foil, net SR≈**−0.62**). Must-teach: persistent trends punish mean reversion via repeated stop-outs.

## Re-estimate thresholds

Train-only quantiles: entry q05≈**−1.84**, exit q50≈**−0.15** vs fixed −2/0. Freeze train estimates for test — do not refit on the full sample.

## Look-ahead warnings

- Rolling z at t may not use t+1 prices.
- Stop fills need an intrabar rule (here: min(open,close) with open=prev close) — pure close-to-close vectors understate gap risk.
- Invalidation list is part of the deliverable, not optional prose.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. No stop and no invalidation discussion.
2. Estimating ±2 on the full sample including test.
3. Claiming MR “works in trends” without a failure foil.
4. Ignoring turnover after whipsaws.
5. Treating stop_exits=0 as proof of safety.
