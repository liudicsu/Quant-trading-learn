## Learning goals

This capstone (~**24 hours**: 6h design/data + 10h backtest/OOS + 4h paper + 4h writing) is an **end-to-end honest small strategy** that stitches every prior phase: hypothesis → PIT data → walk-forward with the **shared cost model** → **DSR counting ALL trials on the same series used for the decision** → risk sizing/limits → execution/TCA estimate → paper plan with **independent-ledger reconciliation** (injected break) and a compliance checklist → a written research-report template, plus a **grading rubric** for *your* pack. Prereqs: `paper-trading-infra`, `compliance-ethics`, `factor-workflow`, `portfolio-risk-sizing`.

**Goals:** deliver a reproducible research pack; include cost sensitivity and sealed OOS; attach paper logs and limitations.

**Must-teach:** grade **honesty and engineering**, not whether the strategy “makes money”; an optional frontier failure appendix must not reopen a sealed hold-out or shrink N.

## Parameters

- Panel: **50 assets × 600** sessions; weak lagged `slow` driver; PIT as-of lag **= 2**
- Shared costs (P2/P4/P8 spirit): **10 bps** one-way + `IMPACT_K=0.1 · σ_lag · √turn`
- Walk-forward: train **240d** / test **60d** / step **60d**; embargo **5**; label horizon **h=1**
- Sealed OOS: last **120** sessions after research window
- Candidate bank **N_TRIALS=6** (includes LEAK/noise — all count)
- Risk: vol-target **10%** ann.; `MAX_GROSS=1.5`; `MAX_NET=0.3`; `DD_KILL=8%`
- TCA toy: notional $1M, ADV $5M, participation 10%; lagged σ for impact
- Paper: frozen `VERSION=capstone_mom10_vt_v1.0.0`; independent broker ledger; one injected partial miss

## Stage map (intuition → formula → number)

### 1) Hypothesis

Write a one-page brief before touching code: claim, null, PIT rule, decision rule, and a “not advice” line. Lab artefact: `examples/p9/data/hypothesis.json`.

**Intuition:** a hypothesis that cannot fail is not a hypothesis.

**Decision rule used here:** keep only if sealed OOS mean daily CS RankIC &gt; 0 **and** DSR(N=all trials) on the **same** net PnL series used for that decision.

### 2) PIT data

Feature at day t may use only information known by close t. Lab builds:

- `slow_lag1` — honest lagged state
- `asof_lag2` — alt-data with reporting lag
- `rev_final_LEAK` — joins a “final” revision as if available earlier
- `slow_raw_noshift_LEAK` — same-bar look-ahead

**Formula (label):** y(i,t) = r(i,t+1) (one-day forward; never fit on y_t with features that peek at t+1).

### 3) Honest walk-forward + shared costs

For each fold, fit/select on train with embargo, evaluate on test with weights **shifted one day** before multiplying returns:

<Formula label="Net PnL with shared costs">
Π(t) = Σ_i w(i,t−1)·r(i,t) − c(t)
c(t) = τ(t)·10bps + 0.1·σ(t−1)·√τ(t)
τ(t) = Σ_i |w(i,t) − w(i,t−1)|
</Formula>

**Numeric (lab):** ≈6 folds per candidate; embargo=5.

### 4) DSR counts ALL trials — same series as the decision

Bailey &amp; López de Prado (2014 JPM): Deflated Sharpe Ratio adjusts the Probabilistic Sharpe Ratio threshold for the expected maximum SR among **N** trials.

**Intuition:** under a zero-edge null, the *best* of N looks lucky; DSR asks whether your selected SR clears that luck threshold.

**Expected-value context:** lab prints DSR(N=6)=**0.000** on the selected net series, while the dishonest foil DSR(N=1) on the **same** series is ≈**0.156**. Inflating confidence by shrinking N is a grading fail.

![Sealed OOS IC by candidate](/p9/capstone_sealed_ic.png)

### 5) Risk sizing / limits

Vol-target the book to 10% ann. with **lagged** proxy vol; enforce gross/net caps and a DD kill. Lab sealed sized path: net_SR≈**−1.79**, maxDD≈**−0.10** → DD kill **would fire** (engineering, not “alpha proof”).

![Sized sealed NAV](/p9/capstone_sized_nav.png)

### 6) Execution / TCA estimate

Perold (1988) implementation shortfall: side × (avg fill − arrival) / arrival. Almgren–Chriss intuition: temporary impact scales like σ·√participation; permanent ≈ half temporary in the toy.

**Lab numbers:** temp≈**37.95** bps, permanent≈**18.97** bps, realized IS≈**8.43** bps + spread 2 + fee 1 → **TCA≈11.43 bps**. Use **ex-ante / lagged** σ — never same-bar realized σ to size impact.

### 7) Paper plan + independent ledger

Freeze `VERSION` for the whole paper window. Rebuild positions from **fill deltas only** on a broker ledger separate from the strategy equity list. Lab injects `INJECTED_PARTIAL_MISS`; reconcile **must** detect abs_diff&gt;0 (date **2025-02-27**, abs_diff=**0.20**). Comparing a series to itself always yields zero and teaches nothing.

![Paper equity](/p9/capstone_paper_equity.png)

### 8) Compliance checklist

Lab CSV checks: learning-only disclaimer; no live orders; PIT documented; **all trials in DSR**; DSR on decision series; version frozen; recon break detected; FINRA Notice **26-10** awareness (PDT successor effective **2026-06-04**); no fabricated citations.

### 9) Research report template

`examples/p9/data/research_report.json` is auto-filled from the run. Your own pack should mirror its sections: hypothesis, data/PIT, costs, trials N, selection, sealed OOS, DSR, risk, TCA, paper/recon, limitations, disclaimer.

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

1. Reporting DSR with **N=1** after shopping many signals.
2. Computing DSR on a different series than the one that drove the keep/reject decision.
3. Joining final revisions / same-bar features (LEAK foils look great; they are cheating).
4. Paper “recon” that compares a ledger to itself.
5. Editing signal rules after seeing paper P&amp;L.
6. Optional frontier appendix that re-opens the sealed year or ignores costs.
7. Treating positive RankIC as proof of edge after costs.
8. Sizing impact with same-bar realized volatility (look-ahead).
