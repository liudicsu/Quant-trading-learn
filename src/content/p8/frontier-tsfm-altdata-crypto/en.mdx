## Base-rate-honest directional accuracy (always-up foil)

[When Directional Accuracy Lies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12248) (Cheung) shows raw ~**80%** directional accuracy on equities can be a **bull-market base rate**: an **always-up** rule scores high with **zero skill**. Headline metric:

`excess_acc` = model accuracy − `always_up` accuracy

on **identical** windows. The paper recreates a legacy high-accuracy condition as a ~**0.70** always-up base the fine-tuned model **fails to beat** (excess negative). Lab bull toy prints always-up in the ~70–80% band and a “marketing mostly-up” model with raw accuracy that can look impressive while **excess ≤ 0**. Short pointer also in `eval-lab`.

<Callout type="warn" title="Reject: “80% accuracy” marketing">
Ads that quote raw directional accuracy without an always-up / excess_acc baseline are base-rate theatre — not forecasting skill.
</Callout>

## TSFM as prior, not alpha engine

[Pretrained TSFMs for Financial Return Forecasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27100) (Noguer i Alonso &amp; Franklin) frames pretraining as an **inductive prior** that can help in low-data per-asset settings, while gains over a random-walk benchmark stay **small and sparse** — not a universal alpha engine. Tie to the base-rate lesson: ranking wins ≠ tradeable edge; reject zero-shot Chronos/TimesFM as “free equity alpha.”

<Callout type="warn" title="Reject: zero-shot Chronos/TimesFM as free equity alpha">
Treat TSFMs as optional priors under purge / costs / DSR — never as a plug-and-play money machine.
</Callout>

## Alt-data PIT / lag

Treat sentiment, filings, web scrapes like any other feature: **as-of timestamps**, revision histories, and embargoes. Reuse the Lopez-Lira post-cutoff idea from the sibling lesson; never join a “final” restated field as if it were available on day 0.

## Crypto PIT audit before alpha

[Point-in-Time Audit Before Alpha](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25348) audits public **Binance BTCUSDT USD-M perpetual** archives with event / publication / availability times. Teaching checklist (lab prints OK rows): **no same-bar execution**; **funding lag** (do not shift realized funding backward); **no backfill** to manufacture continuity; if **OI publication time is unverified**, disable OI as an alpha input. Positive holdout IC still coexisted with **uniformly negative** net Sharpe under their cost grid — audit first, alpha second. Short note also in `data-cleaning`.

## Perpetual funding mechanism — funding arb is NOT risk-free

[Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06888) (He, Manela, Ross, von Wachter) derives no-arbitrage prices / bounds for perpetuals with the exchange **funding** (clamp) mechanism. Longs periodically pay shorts a rate tied to the futures–spot gap (or vice versa) so \(F\) stays near \(S\).

**Critical teaching point:** so-called “funding-rate arbitrage” is **random-maturity**, not classical riskless arb — the gap need not converge on a fixed date, interim mark-to-market can hit margin, and fees/solvency matter.

Lab toy:

```text
premium=20.0bps  funding_8h≈20.0bps on $100,000
  receive ~$200.00 per 8h while gap stays
  if gap widens +60bps before unwind: interim MTM≈$-600.00
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Do NOT ship advertised funding-arb Sharpes as alpha">
Paper strategies can print high sample Sharpes after costs; that is **not** a risk-free edge. Theory + warning only in this course — no recipe tables of advertised Sharpes.
</Callout>

## Liquidation cascades: subcritical engine (reject critical EWS marketing)

[Measuring the engine of a liquidation cascade](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03616) (Garcia Seuma) measures the branching ratio \(\lambda\) **in flight** on Hyperliquid’s October 2025 cascade: \(\hat\lambda \approx 0.1\)–\(0.2\) (**subcritical**). Severity looks like shock × map-in-path × liquidity withdrawal — not a multiplier racing to 1.

Lab Galton–Watson foil: \(\lambda=0.15\), $100M forced → ≈$117.6M amplified (not runaway). **Reject** “critical early-warning → crash” marketing that needs \(\lambda\to 1\).

## Common mistakes / pitfalls

- Zero-shot TSFM on noisy daily excess often loses to strong CS/tree baselines.
- Mock ≠ published Chronos/TimesFM checkpoints.
- Raw ~80% direction without always-up / excess_acc is a base-rate trap (2607.12248).
- Treating TSFM as an alpha engine instead of a prior (2606.27100).
- **Crypto survivorship** and missing PIT audits (2608.25348): same-bar, funding lag, backfill.
- **Funding regime shifts** flip carry; bridges/MEV/oracle/liquidity risks sit outside CEX CCXT.
- Nautilus identical kernel ≠ identical latency, partial fills, or recon outcomes.
- **Scope reject:** Deep RL trading / HFT latency / options MM — out of this curriculum.
